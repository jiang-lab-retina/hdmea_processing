# Radial Center Analysis

## Method

For each feature (and each data type: raw hexbin mean, GAM prediction),
we search for the 2-D center point that maximises the absolute Pearson
correlation between the radial distance from that center and the bin value.

**Search procedure:**
1. Coarse grid: +/-1200 um, step 200 um
2. Fine grid: +/-300 um around coarse best, step 50 um
3. Nelder-Mead optimisation from the fine-grid best

A positive `best_r` means the feature increases with distance from the center
(periphery-high). A negative `best_r` means the feature decreases with
distance (center-high).
